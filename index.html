RURAL STUDENT MOBILITY + DIGITAL CAMPUS

Transportation should never decide a student's future.

ROUTELESS connects students, colleges, government buses and approved private providers into one intelligent system — demand forecasting, fair seat allocation, live safety, attendance, live classes and exams. When buses fail on exam day, it reorganises in seconds and tells the truth about who still needs a seat.

No passwords in this demo — pick a role below. All data is clearly labelled demonstration data. No real GPS, camera or payments are used without your explicit permission.

How it works

STEP 1

Students register

By 8 PM, students book tomorrow's journey — village, pickup, destination, exam or class.

STEP 2

AI allocates seats

The engine matches demand to real vehicle capacity, route and arrival time. Exam students first.

STEP 3

Everyone travels safe

Live journey status, driver contact, emergency help, and arrival tracking for colleges.

STEP 4

Learning continues

If travel fails, students join the live class or exam online — attendance still counts.
